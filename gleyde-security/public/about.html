About

Big-company security, built for Main Street

The cybersecurity and software division of Gleyde Technologies, LLC.

The local businesses people trust most, like the agent handling a family’s home purchase or the lender reviewing their finances, are often protected by the least security.

Large companies have security teams. Small businesses have a website someone built years ago, plugins nobody has updated, and a form that quietly collects sensitive information. Attackers know this.

We exist to close that gap. We audit and harden the sites small businesses already depend on, and build new software that is secure from the start: in writing, only with permission, and in plain English.

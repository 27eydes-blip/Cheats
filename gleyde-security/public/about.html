About Gleyde

Big-company security, built for Main Street

The cybersecurity and software division of Gleyde Technologies, LLC.

Gleyde Technologies started with a simple observation: the local businesses people trust most, like the real estate agent handling a family’s home purchase, the lender reviewing their finances, and the service company with their address and payment details, are often protected by the least security.

Large companies have security teams. Small businesses have a website someone built years ago, a few plugins nobody has updated, and a contact form that quietly collects sensitive information. Attackers know this, and they target small businesses because they are easier to break into.

Our cybersecurity and software division exists to change that. We audit and harden the websites and apps small businesses already depend on, and we build new software that is secure from the start. We work in writing, test only with permission, and explain everything in plain English, because security only works when you understand it.

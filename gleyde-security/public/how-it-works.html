How it works

A simple process, documented at every step

Security testing must be authorized, careful and clear. Here is exactly what to expect when you work with us.

Our four-step process

  1. 1

    Consult

    A free 20–30 minute call. We learn about your business, the website or app you rely on, who manages it, and what you are concerned about. No pressure and no jargon.

    • Understand your goals and any recent incidents
    • Identify which sites, apps and systems matter most
    • Recommend the right starting point and budget
  2. 2

    Written scope & permission

    Before any testing, we send a written scope and authorization agreement. It lists exactly what we will test, what we will not touch, the testing window, and who to contact. Nothing happens until you, as the owner or authorized representative, sign it.

    • Systems in scope and out of scope, by name
    • Testing dates, times and methods
    • Confidentiality and data-handling commitments
    • Fixed price and deliverables
  3. 3

    Audit

    We test only what you authorized, using safe methods designed not to disrupt your business. If we find something critical, we tell you right away instead of waiting for the report.

    • Automated scanning plus hands-on review
    • No changes to your systems during the audit
    • Immediate notice of anything urgent
  4. 4

    Fix & report

    You receive a clear report that ranks each issue by risk and explains it in plain English. We walk you through it, fix the issues if you choose our Hardening service, then re-test to confirm they are closed.

    • Executive summary for owners, technical detail for developers
    • Prioritized fix list with effort estimates
    • Re-test and before/after confirmation
Our commitments

What you can count on

  • Permission first

    We never test a system without a signed authorization from someone who has the right to give it.

  • Confidential by default

    Findings are shared only with you. Data we encounter is handled minimally and deleted when the engagement ends.

  • Plain English

    You will always understand what we found, why it matters, and what it costs to fix.

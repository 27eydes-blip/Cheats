How it works

A simple process, documented at every step

Security testing must be authorized, careful and clear. Here is exactly what to expect.

Our four-step process

  1. 01

    Consult

    A free 20–30 minute call about your business, the site you rely on and what worries you.

    • Your goals and any recent incidents
    • Which systems matter most
    • The right starting point and budget
  2. 02

    Written scope & permission

    Before any testing you get a written agreement. Nothing happens until the owner signs it.

    • What is in and out of scope, by name
    • Testing dates, times and methods
    • Fixed price and deliverables
  3. 03

    Audit

    We test only what you authorized, with safe methods that won’t disrupt your business.

    • Automated scanning plus hands-on review
    • No changes to your systems
    • Immediate notice of anything urgent
  4. 04

    Fix & report

    A clear report that ranks each issue by risk. We fix what you choose, then re-test.

    • Summary for owners, detail for developers
    • Prioritized fixes with effort estimates
    • Before-and-after confirmation

Our commitments

What you can count on

  • Permission first

    We never test a system without signed authorization from someone entitled to give it.

  • Confidential by default

    Findings are shared only with you. Data we encounter is deleted when the work ends.

  • Plain English

    You will always understand what we found, why it matters and what it costs to fix.

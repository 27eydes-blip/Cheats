Questions

Before you ask

Will testing slow down or break my website?

We use careful, non-destructive methods and agree the testing window with you in writing. If we find something urgent, we tell you right away.

Do you need my passwords?

Not for an outside audit. For logged-in areas we ask for a separate test account through a secure channel, and delete it when we’re done.

Is it legal for you to test my site?

Yes, with your written permission. Every engagement starts with a signed scope that names exactly what we may test.

What do I get at the end?

A plain-English report that ranks each issue by risk and explains how to fix it, plus a walkthrough call. With Hardening, we fix the issues and re-test.

We use WordPress, Wix or Squarespace. Can you help?

Yes. Our advice is written for your platform, including what it does and doesn’t let you change.

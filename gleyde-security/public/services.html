Questions

Before you ask

Will testing slow down or break my website?

We use careful, non-destructive methods and agree the testing window with you in writing. If we find something urgent, we tell you right away instead of waiting for the report.

Do you need my passwords?

Not for an outside audit. If you want logged-in areas tested, we’ll ask for a separate test account through a secure channel, never by plain email, and we delete it when we’re done.

Is it legal for you to test my site?

Yes, with your written permission. Every engagement starts with a signed scope and authorization agreement that names exactly what we may test. We never test anything without it.

What do I get at the end?

A plain-English report that ranks each issue by risk, explains why it matters and how to fix it, plus a walkthrough call. If you choose Hardening, we fix the issues and re-test to prove they’re closed.

How much will it actually cost?

The prices on this page are starting points. After a short call you get a fixed, written quote, so you know the full price before any work begins.

We use WordPress, Wix or Squarespace. Can you help?

Yes. We work with the platforms small businesses actually use, and our advice is written for your setup, including what your platform does and doesn’t let you change.

Services & pricing

Four ways we protect your business

Start with an audit, fix what we find, build new software securely, and stay protected. Every engagement begins with a written scope and your permission.

01

Website Security Audit

We examine your website or app the way an attacker would, then give you a clear, prioritized report of what we found and how to fix it.

Who it’s for

Businesses that have never had their website reviewed, are handling client data or online forms, need proof of security for a lender, partner or insurer, or simply want to know where they stand.

Starting price

From $750 per site

Final price depends on site size and number of apps in scope.

What’s included

  • Written scope and authorization before testing
  • Automated and hands-on testing of your site or app
  • Checks for common web vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10)
  • Review of SSL/TLS, security headers, DNS and email settings (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
  • Outdated plugins, themes and software components
  • Login, password and admin-panel exposure
  • Plain-English report with risk ratings and fix steps
  • 30-minute walkthrough call
Request a quote →

02

Security Hardening

We fix the issues found in your audit (ours or someone else’s), then re-test to confirm each one is closed.

Who it’s for

Businesses with a recent audit, a site built on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or custom code, or anyone who has had a scare like spam, a defaced page or a suspicious login.

Starting price

From $1,200 per project

Quoted after the audit, based on the number and type of issues.

What’s included

  • Fixes for the issues in your audit report
  • Software, plugin and theme updates
  • Security headers, HTTPS and cookie settings
  • Admin hardening: strong auth, two-factor, least-privilege accounts
  • Firewall and bot protection setup (e.g. Cloudflare)
  • Backup setup and a tested restore
  • Re-test and before/after summary
Request a quote →

03

Secure Website & App Development

New websites, client portals, intake forms and custom tools, built with security designed in rather than bolted on.

Who it’s for

Real estate teams, lenders and service companies that need a professional site, a secure document upload or intake flow, or a custom internal tool.

Starting price

From $4,500 per project

Simple sites start here; portals and custom apps are quoted by scope.

What’s included

  • Discovery and written project scope
  • Modern, fast, mobile-first design
  • Secure forms with validation and spam protection
  • Security headers, encryption and safe data handling
  • Secrets kept out of code, least-privilege access
  • Pre-launch security review
  • Launch support and handover documentation
Request a quote →

04

Ongoing Monitoring & Updates

Security is not one-and-done. We keep your site updated, watch for problems, and respond quickly when something looks wrong.

Who it’s for

Businesses that want peace of mind without hiring IT staff, and clients who have completed an audit or hardening project and want to stay protected.

Starting price

From $149 per month

Per site. Month-to-month; annual plans available.

What’s included

  • Uptime and SSL certificate monitoring
  • Monthly software, plugin and dependency updates
  • Malware and file-change monitoring
  • Regular backups with periodic restore tests
  • Monthly summary report
  • Priority response if an issue is detected
  • Quarterly security check-in

Privacy

Privacy policy

What this website collects, why, where it goes, and how long it’s kept. We collect as little as we can.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and our own code sets no cookies.
  • The contact form sends your message to us by email. The website doesn’t keep a copy in a database.
  • The free Security Check needs only a website address. Your email is optional.
  • To stop abuse, we count requests per visitor using a salted, one-way hash of your IP address, never the address itself.
  • We never sell or share your information for marketing.

Who we are

This website is run by Gleyde Technologies, LLC (“Gleyde”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers this website, its contact form and its free Website Security Check.

The contact form

When you send the contact form, we receive:

  • your name, email address and message;
  • your website address, if you give one;
  • that you ticked the consent box;
  • the time it was sent, and the country your connection came from (Cloudflare works this out from your IP address; we don’t receive the address itself in the email).

This is sent to our inbox as a plain-text email through our email provider, Resend. We use it only to reply to you and to follow up on your request. The website itself doesn’t store your message in a database.

The form also contains a hidden field and a timer that help us spot automated spam. They don’t collect anything about you.

The free Website Security Check

To run a check, you give us a website address and confirm you’re allowed to check it. You can also choose your industry (for tailored advice) and give an email address (to receive the report).

  • The website you enter. Our server looks at the public parts of that site, the same way a browser would. It also looks up public records about the domain using outside services: Cloudflare DNS, certificate transparency logs (Cert Spotter), domain registration records (RDAP), the OSV vulnerability database, WordPress.org and, if enabled, Google Safe Browsing. These services receive the domain name or software versions, not information about you.
  • The report. The result for a domain is kept for about 15 minutes so repeat checks are fast and the site being checked isn’t overloaded. It is stored against the domain, not against you.
  • Your email, if you give one. We email you the report through Resend, and we receive a copy of the key details (your email, the website, its score, the industry you chose, the platform we detected and the top issues) so we can follow up once. We never share it.
  • Owner verification. If you choose to verify that you own the site, we give you a code made from the domain name. We don’t store it.

Spam and bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

The contact form and the Security Check use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. Turnstile runs a check in your browser and gives the form a one-time token. Our server sends that token and your IP address to Cloudflare to confirm it. Cloudflare handles this information under its own privacy policy.

Rate limits and your IP address

To stop people abusing the forms, we count how often each visitor uses them. We do this with a salted, one-way hash of your IP address (for IPv6, of your network block). The raw address is never stored. Counters expire on their own: about an hour for the contact form and Security Check, and about a day for report emails (which are counted per email address, also hashed).

Cookies and browser storage

Our own code sets no cookies. There are no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers on this site, and our fonts and scripts are served from our own domain.

The Security Check remembers your last few results for each website you check (score, grade and date, up to six per site) in your browser’s local storage, so it can show your progress next time. This stays on your device; it is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser’s settings.

Hosting and logs

This website is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes basic request information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site. If something goes wrong on our server, we log the error so we can fix it; we don’t log the contents of your messages.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell, rent or share your information for marketing. We use these service providers only to run the site:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, security, Turnstile and DNS lookups.
  • Resend: delivering contact-form emails and Security Check reports.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Security Check results: about 15 minutes on our server.
  • Rate-limit counters: about an hour (a day for report emails), then deleted automatically.
  • Emails you send us, and Security Check leads: kept in our inbox while we’re in touch with you, and deleted on request.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law.

To make a request, send us a message and mention “privacy request”. We’ll reply within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

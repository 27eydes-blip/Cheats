Website Security Check

Enter your website and get a scored report in about 15 seconds, with your top fixes and step-by-step instructions. It’s passive: we only look at what any browser or email provider can already see.

Free, no sign-up. If you add your email, we send you the report and may follow up once; we never share it. Limited to a few checks per hour.

What we check

  • HTTPS & certificatesEncryption, http-to-https redirect, HSTS, and when your certificate expires.
  • Security headersProtection against injected code, clickjacking and data leaks, plus software version leaks.
  • Software & page contentOutdated WordPress, PHP and JavaScript libraries with known vulnerabilities, cookie security, insecure forms, mixed content and third-party scripts.
  • Email & domainSPF, DKIM and DMARC against spoofing, DNSSEC, domain renewal date and transfer lock.
  • Exposed filesFiles often left public by mistake, like .env, .git and config backups. We never download or show their contents.

You get a score, your top three fixes, and step-by-step instructions for your platform (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, Cloudflare…). Site owners can verify ownership to unlock deeper checks.

What we don’t do. No logins, no form submissions, no attack payloads, no port scanning, and nothing that could slow down or change your site. Private and internal addresses are blocked.

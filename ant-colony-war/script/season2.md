ANT COLONY WAR — SEASON 2 — "THE FALL OF LORD MANDROS"
Narrator: 250, first person. Lines in [brackets] are the Green speaking.

EPISODE 1 — "THE THRONE"
Six months. That's how long I've ruled the fire colony. Thousands of soldiers, and every one of them lowers their head when I pass. They don't call me 250 anymore. They call me Lord Mandros. The green in my veins is brighter now. Some nights it's the only light in the throne room. The trap-jaws still dig in the cold tunnels. I tell myself it's to keep them safe. Some days I even believe it. This morning, a young trap-jaw broke from the line and ran toward me. My guards grabbed him before he got close. He looked up at me and said, "My mother says you're my brother." His name was Tavi Ren. I didn't even know he existed. And then I heard another name, whispered three tunnels down. Kael Varro. The only friend I ever had. I watched him die the night our colony fell. Apparently... I was wrong.

EPISODE 2 — "THE VOICE"
It started as a hum. Low, in the back of my head, every time the green pulsed. Then one night, alone on the throne, the hum turned into words. [You're welcome.] I froze. [I gave you everything. Your speed. Your senses. Your crown.] I asked what it wanted. [The same thing you want. To never be weak again.] I should've been scared. Instead, I felt understood for the first time in my life. Every ant who ever ignored me, every ant who threw me into the waste chamber, the voice remembered them too. [They will always fear what you are. Let them.] I didn't sleep that night. I didn't need to. The voice kept me company until morning.

EPISODE 3 — "THE SICKNESS"
I started visiting Tavi in secret. I'd bring him the best food in the colony, and he'd tell me stories about our mother. He laughed exactly like I used to, before I forgot how. Then one morning, he didn't laugh. His shell was pale. His antennae barely moved. The healers called it creek rot. They said no ant survives it. I snapped the healer's table in half. The whole chamber went silent. And the voice whispered, [I can save him.] I asked how. [Let me all the way in. No more holding back. No more half of you.] I looked at Tavi, breathing so slow I could count every breath. Seven a minute. Then six. I said, "Do it." [Not yet. First... you have to prove you're mine.]

EPISODE 4 — "KAEL"
He came at night, through a tunnel even I didn't know existed. One broken antenna, a scar across his face, the same crooked smile from the nursery. "Hey, 250." I could've called the guards. I didn't. We sat in the dark like we were kids again. He told me about the rebellion. Two hundred trap-jaws, hiding in an old beetle burrow, waiting. "Free our people," he said. "Come home. This isn't you." For one second, I almost said yes. I almost took off the armor. Then the green pulsed, hot, behind my eyes. [He'll take Tavi. He'll take everything.] Kael stood up to leave and said, "Tomorrow night. The cold tunnels. Be there, brother." Then he was gone. And the voice kept repeating one word. [Tomorrow.]

EPISODE 5 — "THE CHOICE"
The voice showed me things. Tavi in the middle of the rebellion. Fire soldiers flooding the cold tunnels. Tavi trapped in the crowd. [This is what happens if they fight. Your brother dies in their war.] I asked what I should do. [End it before it starts.] I walked to General Velka Thrane's chamber. She'd always watched me like she knew something I didn't. I told her everything. The beetle burrow. The cold tunnels. Tomorrow night. She smiled for the first time since I'd met her. "And the leader?" I hesitated. Just for a second. Then I said his name. "Kael." The green flared so bright it lit the whole chamber. [Good. Now you're mine.] I told myself I was saving Tavi. I told myself a lot of things that night.

EPISODE 6 — "THE PURGE"
I led them myself. Five hundred fire soldiers, moving through the dark, and me at the front. The rebels never had a chance. They were waiting for their friend. Instead, they got Lord Mandros. Every trap-jaw who raised his jaws against me went down. The old worker who fed me in the nursery stood in front of the younger ones and looked at me with tears in his eyes. "Not you, 250. Anyone but you." I didn't answer. I just let the soldiers pass. By morning, the beetle burrow was empty. The rebellion was over. But when I searched every tunnel, one body was missing. Kael. And when I got back to the healing chamber... Tavi's bed was empty too.

EPISODE 7 — "ALL THE WAY IN"
[You did everything I asked.] I demanded to know where Tavi was. [Safe. For now.] And then it happened. The green didn't just pulse. It flooded. Through every vein, every segment, every thought. I collapsed on the throne room floor while thousands of soldiers watched. I felt my old self leaving. Number 250, the crumb carrier, the ant nobody noticed, fading like a scent in the rain. When I stood up, the soldiers backed away. My eyes weren't glowing anymore. They were burning. Then a scout ran in, shaking. "The rebel leader. He's at the creek. And he has a small trap-jaw with him." Tavi. Kael had taken Tavi. [Go,] the voice said. [End him.] For the first time, it didn't feel like the voice was talking. It felt like I was.

EPISODE 8 — "THE BURNING CREEK"
The creek was on fire. The fire ants had burned the banks during the war, and the sap still glowed in the mud like rivers of light. Kael stood on the far bank, Tavi hiding behind him. "Look at yourself," Kael said. "Look what it's done to you." I told him to give me my brother. "He's not safe with you," Kael said. "Nobody is." Then we moved. Two trap-jaws, the fastest jaws in the world, snapping so fast the air cracked. Sparks flew off our shells. We fought across the burning mud, over stones, through smoke so thick I could only see him by his heartbeat. He'd trained his whole life for this. I'd been given it in one night. And for the first time since the amoeba... someone was keeping up with me.

EPISODE 9 — "HIGH GROUND"
We fought until the sun came up. Kael got a snap through my armor. I took one of his legs. Neither of us stopped. Then he climbed. Up the giant root arching over the burning creek. He looked down at me and said, "It's over. Don't follow me." The voice screamed inside me. [He's weak. Take him.] I jumped. I was faster than him. I'd always been faster than him. But faster isn't the same as smarter. Kael snapped at the exact moment I landed. My jaw cracked. My legs gave out. I fell, and the burning mud swallowed half my body. Kael looked down at me, crying. "You were my brother. I loved you." I screamed his name until my voice broke. He picked up Tavi and walked into the smoke. And he never looked back.

EPISODE 10 — "LORD MANDROS" (SEASON FINALE)
The fire soldiers found me at sunset, barely breathing, the green leaking out of my cracks like blood. Velka had them carry me to the deepest chamber in the colony. For nine days, they rebuilt me. Black resin armor, sealed piece by piece over the burns. New mandibles, carved from beetle shell and sharpened like blades. Every piece hurt more than the last. And through all of it, the voice stayed with me. [You don't need them. You only need me.] When it was done, I stood up. Every step was heavy. Every breath sounded like stone grinding. Velka bowed. Then I asked her the only question that mattered. "Where's Tavi?" She looked at the floor. "He's gone. With Kael." I screamed, and the whole colony shook. Thousands of soldiers fell to the ground. Number 250 died in that creek. There's only Lord Mandros now. And somewhere out there, my brother is learning to hate me.

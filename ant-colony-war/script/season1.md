ANT COLONY WAR — SEASON 1
Narrator: Ant 250 (trap-jaw ant), first person. Voice: low, calm, cinematic.

EPISODE 1 — "WAKE"
Dirt. That's the first thing I felt. Cold dirt on my face, and a heartbeat that wasn't slowing down. They told me later I was gone for two days. Dead. But I could hear everything: a beetle digging three tunnels over, a drop of water about to fall. My name is 250. I'm two months old. And before this... I was nobody.

EPISODE 2 — "NUMBER 250"
Three hundred twenty ants in my colony. I was born 250th. Not first, not last, just... in the middle. In the nursery tunnels, nobody learned my name because I didn't have one. I carried crumbs. I followed lines. I was good at being invisible. Then one morning, out past the root wall, I stepped in something that glowed.

EPISODE 3 — "THE GLOW"
It was green, kind of like light you could touch. It crawled up my leg like it knew exactly where it was going. Then it went inside. I remember the burning, and I remember falling. The last thing I saw was the sky, and for the first time I wondered what was up there looking back.

EPISODE 4 — "INVISIBLE"
When I came back, the workers had already carried me to the waste chamber. Ants don't bury their dead, they just throw them out. So I climbed out of a pile of bodies, walked back into the colony, and nobody noticed. Nobody even looked up. And that's when I realized being invisible might be my greatest weapon.

EPISODE 5 — "TESTING"
I started testing it in secret. A falling grain of sand? I could catch it before it hit the ground. A twig the soldiers needed four ants to cut? I snapped through it alone. And when I closed my eyes, I could smell every ant in the tunnels. All 319 of them. Plus something that didn't belong. Something from outside... and it was getting closer.

EPISODE 6 — "THE SCOUT"
Fire ants. A scout from the colony across the creek, hiding in our walls and counting us. I could have warned the queen, but who'd listen to number 250? So I followed him home that night. What I saw there wasn't a colony. It was an army. Thousands of them. And they were all facing our way.

EPISODE 7 — "THE FALL"
I ran home faster than any ant has ever run. I screamed it through every tunnel: they're coming. The guards laughed. A soldier pushed me into the dirt and said, "Go carry your crumbs, 250." By sunrise the walls were burning. I fought. I snapped through twelve of them. It didn't matter. Our queen was dragged out in chains... and I watched.

EPISODE 8 — "CHAINS"
They marched us across the creek on a bridge made of our own dead. Three hundred twenty of us had become one hundred and six. In the fire colony, we weren't ants anymore. We were tools. We dug their tunnels, fed their larvae, and slept in the cold chambers near the surface where the rain came in.

# Glydrift TikToks: captions, hashtags and covers

Ten videos in this folder, all 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, 7–20 s. Each one opens on its most
intense moment and ends with the 1.5 s GLYDRIFT + glydrift.com card. All text stays clear of the
top 150 px and the bottom 450 px. The music is the game's own tracks, and every cut lands on their beat.

The cover for each video is in `covers/NN.png` (1080×1920): pick it in TikTok's **Select cover**,
or upload the PNG. The time noted below is where that frame is in the video, if you'd rather pick it by sliding.

## Posting tips
- Post 1 a day. Start with 01, 09 and 06, which have the strongest hooks.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Pin a comment: "play free at glydrift.com". After the App Store launch, change it to "on the App Store".
- Your own iPhone recordings: `tools/make_tiktok.py` (see `tools/README.md`) turns a screen
  recording into this same style.

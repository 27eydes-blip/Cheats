Support

Need help with Glydrift? Email support@glydrift.com and include your username (Settings shows it). We usually reply within two days.

Common questions

How do I play?
Tap to flap and fly through the gaps. Endless mode takes you through every realm; LEVELS has 50 hand-made levels with three stars each.
Can I play offline?
Yes. Everything works offline; scores and your cloud save upload when you're back online.
How do I keep my progress on a new phone?
Settings → Account → create an account (or Sign in with Apple), then sign in with the same account on the new device.
I bought Drift Pass+ but it isn't unlocked.
Open the Drift Pass and tap RESTORE PURCHASES, signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with.
I forgot my password.
Settings → Account → Sign in → Forgot password. We'll email you a link to set a new one.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete my account. It deletes your account and all its data straight away.
Someone has an offensive name.
Open the leaderboard, tap REPORT A NAME and pick it. It's hidden for you straight away, and names that get reported are removed.
Refunds
Purchases are made through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

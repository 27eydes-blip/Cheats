Privacy Policy

Glydrift (the game at glydrift.com and the Glydrift app for iPhone and iPad). Effective 7 November 2026.

Glydrift is playable without an account. We collect only what the game needs to work: leaderboards, saving your progress, Drift Pass+ and fixing crashes. We don't track you across other apps or websites, we don't show ads, and we never sell your data.

Gameplay statistics (like how far you fly) stay on your device unless you have an account, in which case they're part of your cloud save. A small event log used for game balance is kept only on your device and is never sent.

What we don't collect

No payment details (Apple handles payments), no location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone or camera, and no advertising identifiers. The game has no ads and no tracking, so it never asks for tracking permission.

Who processes it

Our backend is Supabase (database and sign-in). Apple processes App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple. Cloudflare serves the website. These providers process IP addresses and request logs to run their services.

What other players see

Only your username, title, hero, scores, distances, level times and the date. Never your email.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you play. You can delete it all at any time: Settings → Account → Delete my account permanently deletes your account, your cloud save, your leaderboard entries and your purchase records, straight away. Apple keeps its own record of App Store purchases under Apple's privacy policy. Crash reports are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. To delete leaderboard entries made without an account, email us with your username.

Children

Glydrift isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Accounts are optional; if you're under 13, ask a parent before creating one.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Questions or requests: support@glydrift.com.

# DROWNED EARTH — World Bible

The canonical reference for names, places, factions, the hierarchy, story truth and tone. All
game content (`src/data/*.json`) should agree with this file. When they disagree, this file wins.

## Tone guardrails
- Beautiful, quiet, lonely, a little hopeful. Dread through sound, scale and emptiness.
- No gore, no cheap jump scares. Moral gray, not cartoon villains. Every tier of society is human:
  show the hierarchy hurting people, and people at every tier being unexpectedly kind.
- Writing voice: restrained, human, specific. Small concrete details over speeches.

## History in one paragraph
Before the collapse, a deep-sea research project, **Project Undertow**, drilled into something in the
Abyss. The **Sunken Cathedral of Machines** woke. It released a spore-bloom (**the Hush**, an illness
that took the voice first, then the person; ~55% of humanity) and shifted the ocean floor (**the Rise**,
the flood; ~15% more). The Concord knew early and built Highmark first. Since then they have kept the
Cathedral "fed" with salvage and people-hours from the rafts, because shutting it down needs a
sacrifice they refuse to make. The glowing growths are the Cathedral spreading again. The low tone that
shakes the water is its heartbeat. The raft mystics have been half-right all along.

### Stage 1: Lantern Ward, Orrin Bay
Linden Court (start tower), St. Aldren General (sealed Ward 9), Cask Street station (Ferrow Line),
MV Calla Morrow (stranded cargo ship), Vesper House (the lone lit window), **The Sounding** (a collapsed
plaza that drops to an outer spire of the Cathedral), and the raft city **Portage** anchored nearby,
with two small satellite rafts: a Greentide garden barge and a Rusthold rig-tender.

## Highmark
- Ruled by **the Concord**, a council of old families. Public face: **Chancellor Idris Calloway**, calm
  and warm on the radio. Real power: **Director Halden Voss**, head of the Tide Ward and the Archives.
- **Tide Wardens**: patrol boats with searchlights, loudspeakers and boarding crews. Many deckhands are
  raft-born conscripts; some secretly help.
- Rations: sealed crates, **ration seals**, permit stamps. Without permits: no medicine, no fuel.

The player starts **Deep-Born (7)**. Standing gates docks, prices, rations, dialogue tone and patrol
behaviour. Forged tags work until someone checks. Every step up has a cost (Ward service angers rafts,
helping smugglers angers Highmark, a trader permit makes raft-folk call you an informer).

## The player
**Wren Calloway-Ashe** (default; the name is editable), a raft-born repair diver, now Deep-Born. Older
sibling **Teo** was a deep-salvage diver who vanished toward the Cathedral. Teo's recordings are the thread.
(The shared surname with the Chancellor is a thread for a later stage. Wren does not know why.)

## Creatures (only hinted in stage 1)
- **Listeners**: pale, many-limbed shapes that hunt by sound. Stay silent in the dark.
- **Lanternfish swarms**: glow near the Cathedral, go dark when Listeners pass.
- **The Choir**: giant slow shapes whose songs you feel in your chest.
- **Bloomwalkers**: old drowned people overgrown with glowing growth, drifting through submerged streets.
  Uncanny, never gory.

## Secret societies (later stages)
The **Tideholders** (a cult inside the Vigil), the **Salvage Brotherhood** (divers' union), the **Quiet
Hand** (an informant network inside Highmark).

## Currencies
Scrap (everyday), Ration Seals (Highmark-issued, scarce), Salt (raft barter), Glass (rare deep relics).

## Radio
Highmark Official (88.1), Raft Free Voice (pirate, 101.7), The Vigil's Evensong (63.0), Lowlight's coded
channel (3.3), the Night Songs (a lone station that plays old songs at night, 77.7), and static that
sometimes carries impossible voices.

## Endings (main thread: find Teo, 5 acts, one per raft city)
(a) shut the Cathedral down at a personal cost, (b) expose Highmark and unite the rafts, (c) join
Highmark and keep the lie, (d) free the Cathedral and change the world. Each ending reorders the hierarchy.

## Signature set pieces
Lantern-boat festival at dusk on Portage · a fog-bound night under a Tide Ward searchlight · a dive into
the sealed quarantine wing of St. Aldren · a storm at midnight that tears a raft loose · the Beacon
sweeping the world at night with a low answering tone from the Deep.

# Credits

Every third-party asset in Drowned Earth is **CC0 (public domain)**. No attribution is legally required; it is given anyway, with thanks.

Sources are listed in `assets/manifest.json`. `npm run assets:fetch && npm run assets:build` reproduces `public/assets/` from them.

Everything not listed here (architecture, water, sky, clouds, audio, UI, shaders, procedural textures) is original to this project.

## MakeHuman — https://www.makehumancommunity.org (CC0)

The people are built from MakeHuman's system assets, **explicitly released as CC0 by the MakeHuman team in September 2020** (the licence header is in every source file):

- base mesh `hm08`, 454 morph targets (macro age/sex/muscle/weight/ancestry, face and head dials, asymmetry, 34 expression units)
- the default 163-bone skeleton and its skin weights
- skins (21 sets across ages, sexes and ancestries), iris textures (10 colours), eyes (high/low poly)
- eyebrows (12), eyelashes (4), teeth, tongue, hair (afro01, bob01, bob02, braid01, long01, ponytail01, short01–04)
- clothes (male_casualsuit01–06, male_worksuit01, female_casualsuit01–02, female_sportsuit01, shoes01–03/05/06, fedora01), proxy741 (LOD body)

Sources: the core data from `github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman` and the system assets from the public mirror `github.com/vidya-hub/makehuman-core` (unmodified MakeHuman data). `scripts/humans/build.mjs` packs them into `public/assets/humans/`.

Animation for the people is the Quaternius Universal Animation Library (above), retargeted at runtime onto the MakeHuman rig.

## Creatures (the territorial bestiary)

Base models, mutated at load time (smoothed, re-skinned, grown spikes, barnacles, weed and glowing eyes; see `src/creatures/Mutate.js`):

| Species | Base | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Siltwolves (and pups) | `Wolf` — Ultimate Animated Animals | Quaternius | CC0 |
| Gullrats (rats) | `Fox` — Ultimate Animated Animals, re-proportioned into a rat | Quaternius | CC0 |
| Gullrats (gulls) | "Turkey vulture" (rigged and animated by the JGengine authors, Apache-2.0) | Poly by Google, via poly.pizza | CC-BY 3.0 |
| Needlefish, ambient fish | `Fish1` — Animated Fish | Quaternius | CC0 |
| Listeners, Bloomwalkers | MakeHuman base mesh and assets (see below) | MakeHuman team | CC0 |
| Net-Weaver | Procedural (no CC0 jellyfish model exists) | — | — |

The Quaternius and vulture files were taken from the public mirrors in the JGengine
repository (github.com/Noisemaker111/jgengine) and the goblo repository
(github.com/Dustyluc123/goblo); the originals are distributed by Quaternius and poly.pizza.
"Turkey vulture" by Poly by Google is licensed under CC-BY 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); it was recoloured and reshaped into a gull.
